This is an interactive simulation of hypothesis testing. 

[Click here to launch the app in a new window](https://your-shiny-server.shinyapps.io/hypothesis-testing/)

## How to use this app
1. Set the **Null Hypothesis** value.
2. Adjust the **Sample Size** slider.
3. Observe how the **Power** of the test changes.
